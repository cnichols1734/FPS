## Pivot conventions

- Ground props (sandbag, jersey, stall, rubble): base centre, Y-up.
- Wall-mounted (sat dish, window AC, awning): mount point at origin.
- Cables: start endpoint at origin.
- Crossarm: pole centre attach at origin.
